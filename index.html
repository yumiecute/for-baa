hiii baaa,

august 18, 2026

i can't sleep, so ayun, instead of forcing myself to sleep, naisipan kong gumawa nito for you. hindi ko rin alam kung bakit bigla kitang naisip nang ganito ka-late, pero i guess may mga bagay talaga na hindi madaling sabihin nang diretso.

lately, i've been thinking about us again. ang funny lang isipin na sa chats lang talaga tayo unang naging close, instead of face-to-face. parang doon tayo unti-unting naging comfortable sa isa't isa, hanggang sa naging natural na lang yung pag-uusap natin. ngayon grade 10 na tayo, which still feels so weird to think about.

i still remember all the little names we used to call each other — love, baby, mahal, lovelove. ang babaw pakinggan, pero i swear, i miss those little things more than i expected.

01

i remember our first hangout sa ukay-ukay. nothing fancy naman. we were literally just walking around, looking at clothes, talking about random things, and slowly opening up to each other. pero i loved that memory. kasi kahit simple lang, it felt like something that belonged to us.

then yung pag-gala natin sa manila. especially that rainy day. i still remember you getting down on your knees just to wipe the mud off my feet. bumili ka pa talaga ng wipes for me. maybe for you it was just something small, pero sakin, those are the things that stayed. you were always so gentle with me.

you were so giving, caring, mapagmahal, maalaga, and such a gentleman. and i really admired that side of you. especially yung pagiging man of god mo. hindi ko man laging nasasabi, pero i really appreciated that about you.

02

i also remember all those random surprises. yung cravings ko na bigla mong susurprise, and especially during our research defense. chocolates, a flower, jollibee, and that sweater because it was taylor's sweater day. ang dami mong little ways of saying "i care about you" without even having to say it.

and honestly, those are the things i miss. hindi yung grand gestures. i miss the ordinary stuff. yung mcdo. yung random usapan. yung pagtawa natin sa mga bagay na tayo lang makakaintindi. yung feeling na kahit wala naman tayong ginagawa, okay lang. basta ikaw yung kasama ko.

i miss your presence, ba. i miss having you around. i miss having someone who knew me in that very specific way. i miss the version of us na simple lang. walang kailangan patunayan. kahit tayo lang, sapat na.

03

i know we weren't perfect. marami rin tayong hindi napag-usapan nang maayos. minsan hinahayaan lang natin yung problems hanggang lumipas, thinking na maybe magiging okay rin eventually. pero hindi naman pala. naiipon lang.

and now, sometimes when we talk, parang may pieces pa rin ng dati. you still reassure me kapag nagseselos ako. you still talk to me in that familiar way, parang bumabalik tayo sa talking stage natin. and maybe that's why i get confused sometimes. kasi there are moments where it feels like there's still something there, pero minsan hindi ko rin alam kung ako lang ba yung nakakaramdam nun.

i don't want to assume anything from you. ayoko rin namang pilitin kang sabihin yung gusto kong marinig. i just want to understand you. i'd rather know what you genuinely think and feel kaysa lagi akong nanghuhula sa meaning ng mga little things.

kasi if i'm being honest, i still miss you. sobra.

i miss the way you cared for me. i miss your presence. i miss the little things. i miss being "us." and kahit ayokong aminin minsan, there's still a part of me that wants you back.

pero if ever man na magkaroon ulit ng "us," ayokong bumalik lang tayo sa dati without learning anything. i'd want us to talk more. to actually say what hurts. to stop letting things pile up. i want something that feels like us again, pero this time, something healthier.

and at the same time, i still want that simple version of us. yung pwede tayong mag-mcdo, magkwentuhan ng walang katapusan, gumawa ng random memories, and somehow feel like that's enough.

i don't know exactly what you think about all of this. and i'm not writing this to force anything from you. i just wanted you to know. this is where my heart has been lately.

and maybe someday, if you feel the same, i'd be happy to know. not because i expect it, but because i'd rather hear it from you than keep wondering.

for now, i just want to say thank you. thank you for being here for me again. thank you for still being someone i can talk to. thank you for all the memories, all the little things, and everything you once gave me. i'll always be grateful for those.

you became such a big part of my life, ba. and even after everything, i still care about you more than i probably should.

i still miss you. and maybe a little part of me is still hoping.

-from your most gorgeous cutiepie
